Lines should not be too long.

Why is this an issue?

Having to scroll horizontally makes it harder to get a quick overview and understanding of any piece of code.

Code examples

Non-compliant code example

_method exemplar.example_method()
    _return "This is a very long line that goes on and on and on and on and on and on and well past the recommended line length limit"
_endmethod

Compliant solution

_method exemplar.example_method()
    _local message << "This is a very long line that goes on and on and on and on " +
                       "and on and on and well past the recommended line length limit"
    _return message
_endmethod
