XXX/TODO/FIXME comments should be resolved.

Why is this an issue?

XXX/TODO/FIXME comments mark work that was intentionally left unfinished. Left unresolved, they accumulate silently and are easy to forget, so the gap or workaround they describe can end up shipping to production.
